    Questions

    What buyers ask first

    Is this a certification?

    No. We aren't a certification body, and the report doesn't certify compliance with SOC 2, ISO, HIPAA or any law. It's an independent assessment of specific claims, with evidence and limits written down.

    Why not build the eval internally?

    You should. But your buyer asks who checked it. An internal eval answers "can we measure it"; an independent one answers "should you believe it".

    Who have you done this for?

    No one yet. io threads is new, and we'd rather say so. That's why the iot API is free to call: judge the work before you pay for it.

    What if the verdict is bad?

    Then you hear it from us before your buyer does, with what to fix. A FAIL in a Diagnostic is the cheapest one you'll get.

    What access do you need?

    Usually the system and a sample of real inputs. The Diagnostic works out the smallest access that answers the question.

    How is enterprise priced?

    Per seat, with credits pooled across the team and invoiced monthly or yearly. Every engagement is scoped and priced in writing before work starts.

    Can you work under our security rules?

    Yes. NDA first, the least access that answers the question, testing only in environments you authorize, and nothing used to train a model.

    Enterprise and partnerships

    Seat licenses, pooled credits, invoicing and a named contact. Partner seats are limited.
