Privacy, plainly. This page is a demo: replies are pre-recorded and no model is downloaded. Sessions, settings and receipts stay in this browser. If you choose to run a real model here, its files download from Hugging Face the first time, which shows them your IP address. Small models make mistakes. A receipt proves this browser's key signed the record, not that the record is true. Want agents that work with your files, phone approvals or a team host? Talk to io threads.
